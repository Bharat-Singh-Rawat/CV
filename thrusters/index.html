Electric propulsion in space: every flown ion and Hall thruster

Each spacecraft (or series, or constellation) that has flown a gridded ion engine or a Hall-effect thruster, placed on its orbit around an animated Earth. Blue: gridded ion engines (accelerating grids, ion beam). Violet: Hall thrusters (annular channel, plasma plume). Click a spacecraft or a list entry for its thruster, the thruster's key parameters and the sources.

Thruster models and key parameters

Nominal operating point unless noted; ranges show the throttle range. Ion engines: beam voltage = energy per singly charged beam ion (eV). Hall thrusters: discharge voltage and current. Every value links to its source; empty cells were not found in a citable source.

About the data

Compiled from agency, manufacturer and peer-reviewed sources (IEPC, AIAA and journal papers), listed on every entry. Constellations are shown as one entry tagged cluster; families of near-identical satellites as one entry tagged series. Positions: spacecraft still in orbit are at their real current position, propagated with SGP4 (satellite.js) from CelesTrak orbital elements refreshed daily; retired spacecraft fly their catalogued orbit (CelesTrak SATCAT inclination, perigee, apogee; for decayed low orbits the final pre-decay altitude is replaced by 450 km); spacecraft without a catalogue match use a representative orbit for their class. Constellations show a sample of 240 of their real satellites. Deep-space trajectories: JPL Horizons. Sun and Moon positions and Earth's rotation are computed for the displayed time. The Overview scale compresses distances (log-like); the True scale buttons show real distances. Images: Earth day map NASA Goddard (Blue Marble; R. Stöckli, R.
